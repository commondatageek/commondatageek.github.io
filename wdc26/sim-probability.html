What are the odds of finishing 10 items in 20 days?

Replay a fixed number of random days and check whether the goal was met. The share of futures that made it is our probability.

Tip: click "Draw a day" a few times by hand first.

Order doesn't matter: this is a bag of days. Every day is equally likely to be drawn, and a drawn day goes back in the bag (sampling with replacement). Weekends are in the bag too: some have a completion or two, most have none, just like real life.
